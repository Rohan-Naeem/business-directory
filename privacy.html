Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Global Bizz Hub values your privacy. This policy outlines how information submitted through our directory forms and website visits is processed.

1. Public Commercial Data

Information submitted as part of a business listing (including commercial business name, public address, business telephone number, and website) is made available on public profile pages to assist consumers and clients in contacting the company.

2. User Reviews & Reports

User feedback and moderation reports are reviewed to protect the directory from abuse and spam. Reviewer email addresses are kept confidential and are not sold to third-party marketing lists.

3. Cookies & Analytics

Global Bizz Hub uses standard functional browser storage (such as localStorage) to preserve your search preferences and session states.
