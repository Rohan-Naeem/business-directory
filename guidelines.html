Listing Submission Guidelines

To maintain directory quality and search engine trust, all business submissions must meet the following eligibility criteria:

1. Real Physical or Operating Presence

Businesses must have a verifiable physical address, official commercial office, clinic, or verified service area within Pakistan, the United States, or the United Kingdom. Purely virtual dummy websites or lead capture farms are strictly prohibited.

2. Accurate Direct Contact Information

All submissions must provide an active telephone number and valid email address. Toll-free numbers or official business WhatsApp lines are encouraged when appropriate.

3. Original Descriptions (No Keyword Stuffing)

Descriptions should accurately describe the enterprise's products, history, and scope of operations. Automated keyword repetitions and spam links will lead to immediate rejection.
