Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding listing submissions, verification standards, and directory indexing.

How do I find a business on NexusBiz?
Use our global search bar on the homepage or search page. You can filter by business keyword, industry category, country, or specific city to narrow down results.
Is it free to submit a business listing?
Yes, standard listings are free. All submissions undergo administrative moderation to ensure contact accuracy and compliance with directory standards.
What does the "Verified" badge mean?
A verified badge indicates our directory team has confirmed the physical address, valid phone number, and operating status of the registered organization.
Can I update or modify my business information later?
Yes, listing owners can contact directory support or submit an update request with official verification documents.
Which countries are currently supported?
We currently cover Pakistan (PK), the United States (US), and the United Kingdom (UK), with automated geographic expansion tools for additional regions.
